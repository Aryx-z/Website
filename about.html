01. Our Belief

Revenue data should be honest, immediate, and undeniable.

We reject vanity metrics, estimated pipelines, and dashboards that make bad months look good. Stratum surfaces reality — with the precision of a transit map and the clarity of a stop sign. No noise. No decoration. Just truth.

02. Our Values

What We Stand For

01 — Objectivity
Data Over Opinion

Every feature we ship must help users see reality more clearly. We will not build features that help users avoid the truth.

02 — Clarity
No Hidden Complexity

If a metric requires explanation, it's probably wrong. We simplify until the answer is self-evident and the path is clear.

03 — Precision
Earn Every Number

Estimates and projections are labeled as such. Actuals are actuals. We never confuse the two, and neither should your team.

04 — Speed
Time Kills Deals

Insights must arrive before decisions, not after. Stratum is built for the moment your team needs to act — not the next morning's report.

03. Our History
